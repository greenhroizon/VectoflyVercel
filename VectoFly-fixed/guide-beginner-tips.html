Beginner tips: 12 ways to fly farther

By the VectoFly team · Updated 11 November 2026

VectoFly looks simple: slide up or down and dodge. Yet the first few flights often end quickly, because the game is built to reward habits rather than reflexes. These twelve tips come from how the game actually works, and each one removes a common reason for crashing early. If you have not read the basics yet, start with How to play.

1. Start on Easy and keep your first plane

Easy gives a slower start, lighter damage and fewer hazards. Its score multiplier is x1, so you are not “wasting” runs: you are learning how waves are built. Move up to Medium (x1.25) once you reliably pass level 3 or 4. The free Classic plane has no perks, which is a good thing at first, because you learn exactly how the game behaves without help.

2. Look at the gap, not at the spikes

Your eyes go where you look. Most obstacles come as walls with a gap (a “gate”), scattered groups or combs, and there is always a way through. Find the gate early and steer toward the opening instead of staring at the red spikes you want to avoid. The game’s obstacle director keeps the next gate only a lane or two away from the previous one, so a calm, small correction is usually enough.

3. Make small moves early, not big moves late

A long last-moment swerve is how plane and spike meet. Because the plane moves by the amount you slide, tiny thumb movements are precise. As soon as a wave appears, nudge toward its gate and then hold still.

4. Never park at the top or the bottom

The edges feel safe, but the game punishes standing still. If you stay at one height for a few seconds, an orange homing spike is called to that height. Keep drifting gently through the middle of the screen so you are never an easy target.

5. Learn the flashing “!” warning

A flashing ! at the edge of the screen tells you where a homing spike is coming from. Do not react immediately: homing spikes follow your height and only lock on shortly before impact. Keep moving, then dodge at the last moment, in the opposite direction from where it is aiming.

6. Treat blue pills as repairs, not trophies

A blue pill repairs damage and gives 5 points. If your damage bar is nearly full, a pill is worth a small detour. If the bar is empty, ignore pills that sit near spikes. Pill trails often sweep up and down on purpose, to tempt you into danger; take them when the route is clear.

7. Watch fuel as carefully as damage

Fuel drains slowly while you fly, and a run also ends when the tank is empty. Orange fuel cans refill it. Do not let the gauge fall into the red before you look for a can. Planes that use less fuel (Paper Plane, Phantom Glider, Sky Balloon) make this easier, and the free Comet Floatplane makes each can refill 50% more.

8. Build combos only when it is safe

Every five pills in a row without being hit gives a bonus of 3 coins, and passing a spike by a hair gives a “close call” bonus of 1 coin (up to 15 per run). These are nice extras, but a crash costs far more than the bonus. Collect pills that are on your path and skip the ones that are not.

9. Pick the free plane that fixes your biggest problem

  • Crashing a lot: Tank Bomber takes 40% less damage.
  • Running out of fuel: Comet Floatplane (cans refill 50% more) or Paper Plane (15% less fuel use).
  • Missing pills: Falcon has a big pill magnet.

Compare them on the planes page.

10. Use the pause and the safe maps

Press P (or use the pause control) to take a breath. For practice, choose a map whose only threats are birds and wind gusts; the maps page lists them. You can focus on spikes without fighters or lasers asking for attention as well.

11. Keep sessions short

Tired thumbs make sloppy moves. Five or ten minutes of focused flying beats an hour of frustration. Your coins and unlocks are saved on your device, so stopping loses nothing.

12. Collect the daily gift

Open the game once a day. The daily gift starts at 20 coins and grows with each day in a row, which pays for your first new plane faster than any single run. See the coins guide for the details.

A ten-minute practice plan

  1. Three runs on Easy with the Classic plane. Only goal: reach level 3.
  2. Three runs with the Falcon, collecting every pill that is safe. Notice how the magnet helps.
  3. Three runs on Medium. Practise dodging homing spikes at the last moment.
  4. One run just for fun on any map you like.

After a week of this routine most players are comfortable on Hard. When you want to chase the world leaderboard, continue with the high-score strategy guide.
