Privacy Policy

Updated 31 October 2026

Information on your device

VectoFly stores your pilot name, device-generated player ID, best scores, wallet, purchased unlocks, selected aircraft and map, and settings in browser local storage. Clearing browser data can remove this progress. No account or password is required.

Global leaderboard and security

When the score service is available, your chosen pilot name, best score, distance, level, aircraft, difficulty and submission time are stored by the leaderboard service. Pilot names and best scores are public. A hashed player key identifies your entry publicly; your device ID is sent to the score server. Choose a nickname, not a real name or email. The service also counts devices and game starts and uses signed run tickets and rate limits to reduce abuse. Hosting providers receive connection information such as IP addresses and browser request metadata.

Optional Google Analytics

Only after you allow analytics, Google Analytics 4 (G-LXSLC7ZVNW) measures page views, game starts and ends, selections and leaderboard visits. Google may process cookies, device and browser information, approximate location and usage data. We do not send pilot names, email addresses or player IDs as custom analytics events. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in our configuration. Use Analytics preferences to change your choice; declining stops future loading after reload but does not delete previously collected data or existing Google cookies. You can clear those through browser settings.

Other providers

The website is designed for Vercel hosting. Its score function uses Firebase Realtime Database when configured; the alternative bundled Node server stores scores in files. The game loads the CrazyGames SDK, which may provide platform integration, cloud saves and ads where available. Those services may process information under their own policies. Google Analytics policies are available at policies.google.com/privacy and CrazyGames policies at crazygames.com/privacy-policy. We do not claim control over third-party processing.

Retention, requests and children

Local saves remain until cleared. The current score service retains up to 5,000 ranked entries; operational retention depends on the hosting configuration. Email contact@vectofly.com with your pilot nickname and approximate score for access, correction or removal requests. Do not email passwords or private keys. Rights depend on applicable law; identity verification may be necessary. This is a general-audience game. A parent or guardian may contact us about information supplied by a child. No online service can guarantee complete security.
