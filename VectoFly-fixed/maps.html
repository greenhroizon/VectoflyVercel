All maps in VectoFly

By the VectoFly team · Updated 11 November 2026

VectoFly has 50 maps. Every map changes the scenery, the sky colours as you climb, and, importantly, the threats you must dodge on top of the red spikes. The Sunny Coast, Sunset Bay, Tropical Lagoon and Red Canyon are free from the start; the others are bought with coins in the Maps menu.

Every threat shows a warning before it hits, so you can always dodge it. To learn how each one behaves, read the hazards guide.

Picking a map

Easier maps for learning

Maps whose only special threats are birds and wind gusts never shoot at you and never drop anything on you. Good choices include Sunny Coast, Sunset Bay, Tropical Lagoon, Red Canyon, Flamingo Marsh, Autumn Valley, Cherry Blossom Bay, Tulip Fields. Birds do about half the damage of the other hazards, and wind gusts push you without hurting you at all.

Harder maps for a challenge

Maps with fighters, saucers, bombers or lasers keep you moving. These include Warzone Skies, Neon Night, Air Raid City, Ace Alley, Metro City, Canyon Run, Pyramid Plateau, Laser Lab, Medieval Kingdom, Cyber Grid, Mars Colony, Moonlit Sea, Deep Space, Alien Skies. Lasers, bombers and aimed shots demand the fastest reactions.

Scenery and mood

If you simply want to relax, try the pastel Candy Clouds, the cherry blossoms of Cherry Blossom Bay, the always-night Moonlit Sea or the lavender fields. Because the sky changes colour as the level rises, even a familiar map looks different every time you reach new heights.
