Cookie & Storage Policy

Updated 31 October 2026

Essential game storage

Browser local storage preserves progress, purchases made with earned coins, settings and a device-generated player ID. A separate preference records your analytics choice. These features do not require optional analytics.

Optional analytics and external services

Google Analytics is loaded only after you choose Allow analytics. It may use cookies to measure visits and gameplay. You can decline or change your choice using Analytics preferences on any page. Previously stored cookies can be removed in browser settings. The CrazyGames SDK and hosting services may have their own storage practices; see our Privacy Policy. Blocking storage may prevent progress from being saved.
