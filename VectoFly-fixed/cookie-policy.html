Cookie Policy

This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage used by vectofly.com. For how we handle personal information in general, read the Privacy Policy.

What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar browser feature that keeps data on your device. Both let a site remember things between visits.

What VectoFly uses

NameTypePurposeLifetime
vectofly* (local storage)Strictly necessary / functionalSaves your coins, unlocked planes, paints and maps, difficulty, sound/vibration settings, personal bests, pilot name and a random player ID for the leaderboardUntil you clear site data

We do not currently use advertising or social-media tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this page first and ask where the law requires.

Your choices

  • Switch analytics off for this browser:
  • Browser settings: you can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser. The Game still works without analytics cookies, but without local storage it cannot remember your progress.
  • Google's opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Delete your game data: clear this site's data in your browser settings.
