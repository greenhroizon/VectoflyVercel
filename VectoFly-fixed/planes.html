All planes in VectoFly

By the VectoFly team · Updated 11 November 2026

VectoFly has 32 planes. They all fly the same way, but each one comes with perks that change how a run feels: some take less damage, some pull in pills from further away, some sip fuel and some earn extra coins. Below is every plane in the game with its price and exact perks, followed by advice on which one to choose for your goal. For how the coins themselves work, read the coins and unlocks guide.

What the perks mean

  • Armor reduces the damage you take from spikes and hazards. “40% less damage” means a hit fills your damage bar only 60% as much.
  • Pill magnet widens the area from which blue pills are pulled to your plane, so you collect pills (repairs and +5 points each) without chasing them. A value of x2 roughly doubles that reach.
  • Fuel use lowers how quickly the fuel gauge drains, so you can fly farther between orange fuel cans.
  • Coins multiplies the coins you earn from the pills you collect in a run (see the formula in the coins guide).
  • Fuel cans makes each orange can refill more of the gauge.

Perks never touch the scoring rules: score is still distance plus pills times the difficulty multiplier. A better plane simply helps you stay alive and earn coins faster. Skill and practice matter more than any plane.

Which plane should you choose?

To survive longer (armor)

  • Space Shuttle (5,200 coins): 50% less damage
  • Tank Bomber (free): 40% less damage
  • Sky Dragon (3,000 coins): 40% less damage
  • Royal Zeppelin (4,000 coins): 40% less damage

The free Tank Bomber is the best armor you can have on day one, with 40% less damage, which is why many players keep it long after they could buy something fancier.

To earn coins faster

  • Royal Zeppelin (4,000 coins): 2.5x coins
  • Golden Jet (1,500 coins): 2x coins
  • Space Shuttle (5,200 coins): 2x coins
  • Flying Taxi (750 coins): 1.5x coins

Coin bonuses stack with the difficulty multiplier, so a coin plane on Hard or Hell is the quickest way to afford the next unlock.

To save fuel

  • Sky Balloon (2,900 coins): 45% less fuel use
  • Ornithopter (1,050 coins): 40% less fuel use
  • Jetpack Hero (3,600 coins): 40% less fuel use
  • Space Shuttle (5,200 coins): 40% less fuel use

To collect pills without risk (magnet)

  • Saucer (3,200 coins): magnet x2.2
  • Jetpack Hero (3,600 coins): magnet x2.2
  • Night Bat (2,300 coins): magnet x2
  • Royal Zeppelin (4,000 coins): magnet x2

The free Falcon has a magnet of x1.8, the biggest magnet you can use from the start. It is a great way to learn pill trails without flying into danger.

Paints

Besides planes there are 65 paints in the Hangar: plain colours and patterns such as stripes, camouflage, flames, galaxy and rainbow. Paints are purely cosmetic. They cost coins but do not change any perk, so buy them last.

A sensible shopping order

  1. Fly with the free planes and find the one that fits your style (Falcon for pills, Comet Floatplane for fuel, Tank Bomber for armor, Paper Plane for low fuel use).
  2. Save for one plane with a coin bonus, such as the Flying Taxi (1.5x coins) or Golden Jet (2x coins), so later unlocks come faster.
  3. Then buy an armor plane for the difficulty you play most.
  4. Keep the expensive legends, such as the Royal Zeppelin and Space Shuttle, as long-term goals.
