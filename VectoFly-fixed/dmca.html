Copyright & DMCA Notices

Updated 31 October 2026

Report an infringement

If you believe material on https://vectofly.com infringes your copyright, email contact@vectofly.com. Identify the copyrighted work, the specific URL and material you object to, your contact details, and the basis of your claim.

Notice details

Where applicable to your notice, include a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent or law; a statement that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act (under penalty of perjury where required); and a physical or electronic signature. Do not send knowingly false notices.

Review and response

We will review sufficiently detailed notices and may restrict material while investigating. If you believe content was removed in error, contact us with the relevant details and any rights or authorization. This page is a contact process, not a claim of registered DMCA-agent status or a guarantee of statutory safe-harbor eligibility.
