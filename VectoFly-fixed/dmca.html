DMCA and Copyright

Last updated: November 1, 2026

VectoFly respects the intellectual property of others and expects its users to do the same. VectoFly does not host user-uploaded files, music or images - the only per-player content is a randomly generated pilot name and a score. If you believe something on vectofly.com infringes your copyright (including a pilot name or text), tell us and we will act quickly.

Sending a takedown notice

Email contact@vectofly.com with the subject "Copyright notice" and include:

  1. your name, address, email and phone number, and your electronic or physical signature;
  2. a description of the copyrighted work you say is infringed (with a link if it is online);
  3. the exact URL or a clear description of where the allegedly infringing material is on our site;
  4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
  5. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

We may remove or disable the material, may tell the person who posted it, and may forward your notice (with your contact details) to them.

Counter-notice

If material you provided was removed by mistake or misidentification, email us a counter-notice with: your name, address, email and signature; a description of the removed material and where it appeared; a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification; and your consent to the jurisdiction of the courts for your address. If the claimant does not start legal action within 10-14 business days, we may restore the material.

Repeat infringers

We may block users who repeatedly infringe copyright.
