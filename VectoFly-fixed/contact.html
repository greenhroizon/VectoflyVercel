Contact & Feedback

Updated 31 October 2026

Get in touch

Email contact@vectofly.com for questions, bug reports, suggestions, privacy requests or copyright concerns. This link opens your mail app; VectoFly does not submit a hidden feedback form.

Report a bug

Tell us what happened, what you expected, your browser and device, portrait or landscape orientation, and steps to reproduce. Screenshots can help. Please avoid sending passwords, credentials or unnecessary personal information.

Scores and saves

For leaderboard issues include your public nickname and approximate score. Local saves belong to your browser and may not be recoverable once cleared. Response times vary; we do not promise round-the-clock support.
