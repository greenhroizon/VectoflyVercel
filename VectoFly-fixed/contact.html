Contact VectoFly

We are a small team and we read every message. Write to contact@vectofly.com. We usually reply within 2-3 working days (privacy requests within 30 days).

Report a bug / send feedback

That button opens your email app with your device details (browser, screen size, game version) filled in - they help us reproduce the problem. You can delete them if you prefer.

Found a bug?

Please tell us:

  • what you expected and what happened instead (a screenshot or short video helps a lot);
  • your device and browser (for example "iPhone 13, Safari" or "Samsung A14, Chrome");
  • the map, plane and difficulty you were using, and what you did just before it happened.

Cheaters and exploits: if you find a way to submit fake scores, please email us privately rather than posting it publicly.

Feedback and ideas

New planes, maps, features, control tweaks, things that feel too hard or too easy - we want to hear it. The best ideas end up in the game.
