Voters turn against data centers: 40 of 41 local ballot measures go against them

Ballotpedia's count, cited by Reuters, shows a sharp public backlash against AI infrastructure build-outs.

In the US midterm elections, 40 of 41 data-center-related ballot measures went against the projects, according to Ballotpedia figures cited by Reuters. Reuters also pointed to winners in Texas and Georgia who campaigned on slowing expansion.

Local concerns centre on electricity grids, water use, land and the number of permanent jobs a facility creates once construction ends. The result lands as tech companies race to secure computing capacity for AI services.
