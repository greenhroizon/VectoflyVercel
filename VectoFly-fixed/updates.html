1.5 - Guides, catalogs and an ad-free site

11 November 2026 · Version 1.5

New

  • Seven written guides: beginner tips, hazards, coins and unlocks, difficulty and scoring, controls and performance, high-score strategy and how the game is built.
  • A full planes catalog (all 32 planes with prices and exact perks) and a maps catalog (all 50 maps with their hazards).
  • A FAQ with answers about saving, coins, scores and performance.

Website

  • VectoFly shows no ads. The privacy and cookie policies were updated to say so.
  • The main menu links on every page now include Planes, Maps and Guides.

1.4 - Faster, smoother and fully independent

7 November 2026 · Version 1.4

Improved

  • Faster start. The game now starts as soon as its own files are ready instead of waiting for every other script on the page, and a loading screen shows until the first frame is drawn. Slow connections no longer look frozen.
  • Automatic graphics quality. If the game runs slowly it turns off shadows first, then lowers the resolution, and remembers the setting for next time. Phones with little memory start in a lighter mode.
  • Low graphics option in the menu for a smoother game on older phones.
  • Clear pause notice. A readable "Paused" box appears on every map. Press P, or click or tap the game, to resume.
  • VectoFly now runs only on its own website. The CrazyGames SDK was removed, so there are no ads before a run and progress is saved in your browser only. The privacy policy was updated to match.

Fixed

  • A damaged or edited save file could stop the game from starting. Saved data is now checked on load and anything wrong falls back to safe defaults.
  • Full test pass on desktop and phones: every page, all 32 planes, all 50 maps and their hazards, the Hangar, the controls and the leaderboard server were checked.

Website

  • New Updates page (this one).

1.3 - Hazards, 10 new planes and 11 new maps

4 November 2026 · Version 1.3

New

  • Every map now has its own threats on top of the red spikes: enemy fighters and alien saucers that shoot at you, bombers, meteors, lightning, laser beams, fireballs, falling icicles, bird flocks and wind gusts. Each one shows a warning first, such as a flashing nose, a red path or a ring in the sky. Easy gets them less often and Hell more often.
  • 10 new planes: Spitfire, Rising Sun, Flying Taxi, Sky Sub, Ornithopter, Harrier, Supersonic, Night Bat, Sky Dragon and Jetpack Hero. That makes 32 planes in total.
  • 11 new maps: Ace Alley, Warzone Skies, Air Raid City, Meteor Shower, Flamingo Marsh, Thunder Plains, Canyon Run, Frozen Spires, Laser Lab, Dragon’s Peak and Alien Skies. That makes 50 maps, and the Maps screen lists the threats of each one.
  • The game-over screen shows how long you played, for example "You played 1 min 52 sec".

Improved

  • A hand-made menu look that matches the game: a pilot licence card with your stats, a single difficulty bar, a stamped "New best" result and a centred title. The old tagline was removed because obstacles now change from map to map.
  • The menu fits every screen size: two columns on wide screens and one column on phones, with no scrolling needed to reach Play.
  • The in-game score, level, damage and fuel text is crisper and easier to read.

Fixed

  • A blank strip at the bottom of the game on some phones. The game now fills the whole screen and follows rotation and browser-bar changes.
  • Enemies, meteors and fireballs are scaled for narrow phone screens so they always stay in view.
  • The wind-gust message appeared twice.

Website

  • The home, How to play and About pages and the FAQ now describe the new planes, maps and hazards.

1.2 - Matching leaderboards and random pilot names

1 November 2026 · Version 1.2

New

  • Random pilot names. Every player gets a name like "Swift Falcon 42", and a New name button gives you another. Typing your own name is no longer possible, so the leaderboard never shows personal information. The game servers only accept names in this format.
  • A new plane icon and favicon for browsers, home screens and installed apps.

Improved

  • The scoreboard is no longer shown while you fly. The world leaderboard is in the menu, with gold, silver and bronze tags for the top 3, your own row highlighted and a line showing your world rank. The full list is on the Leaderboard page.
  • The menu shows your best score, world rank, flights and top level at a glance, and a results card after each run.
  • The menu is compact on big screens instead of filling them.

Fixed

  • The in-game and menu leaderboards did not always match: fake practice rows could appear, your own entry could show twice, and a new best was not shown right after saving. All boards now use the same list and update at once.
  • The leaderboard list could open scrolled down and hide ranks 1 to 3.

Website

  • Privacy, terms, DMCA, contact and leaderboard pages were updated for random names.

1.0 - VectoFly launches

Late October 2026 · Version 1.0

New

  • An endless 3D flying game that runs in your browser with no download and no sign-up: dodge obstacles, collect pills and fuel and climb the levels.
  • Four difficulty levels, a Hangar with planes and paints, maps to unlock with coins, daily gifts, and a world leaderboard.
  • Play on phones, tablets and computers, and install it to your home screen.

Dates are release dates. Small fixes may be made between versions.
