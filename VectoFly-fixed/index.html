    VectoFly - Free Endless 3D Plane Game

    VectoFly is a free online flying game built with WebGL. Choose a plane, fly over an endless world, repair your aircraft with blue pills, refuel with orange cans and dodge the red spikes for as long as you can. Every run counts toward the global leaderboard.

    How to play VectoFly

    Steer with the mouse, touch, arrow keys or WASD. Press Space to start, P to pause and M to mute. Collect blue pills to repair damage and orange fuel cans to keep flying. If your damage bar fills or you run out of fuel, the run is over.

    Game features

    • Endless 3D flying with a speed that rises as you level up
    • Hangar with multiple planes and paint patterns
    • Several maps and four difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Hell
    • World leaderboard (top 50 in the menu, every pilot on a full leaderboard page), with a random pilot name for every player
    • Clean game screen: no scoreboard while you fly, with your rank, best score and results shown in the menu
    • Works worldwide on desktop, tablet and mobile browsers, no download

    Frequently asked questions

    What is VectoFly?

    VectoFly is a free endless 3D plane game that runs in your browser. You fly over an endless world, avoid red spikes, collect blue repair pills and orange fuel cans, and try to reach the top of the global leaderboard.

    Is VectoFly free to play?

    Yes. VectoFly is completely free to play online. There is nothing to download or install and no account is required.

    How do I play VectoFly?

    On a phone or tablet, slide a thumb anywhere on the screen to steer. On a computer, move the mouse or use the arrow keys or WASD. Press Space to start, P to pause and M to mute. Collect blue pills to repair damage and orange cans to refuel.

    Can I play VectoFly on mobile?

    Yes. VectoFly works on phones, tablets and desktop computers in any modern browser that supports WebGL, using touch controls on mobile.

    Does VectoFly have a leaderboard?

    Yes. The menu shows the top 50 pilots in the world, and the full leaderboard page lists every pilot with search and your own rank. Each pilot keeps one best score and gets a randomly generated pilot name.

    Can I choose my own pilot name?

    No. Every pilot gets a randomly generated name such as Swift Falcon 42, so nobody can type their own and no personal information ends up on the leaderboard. Use the New name button in the menu to get a different random name.

    Are there different planes and maps?

    Yes. The first five planes and first four maps are free, and in the Hangar you can unlock more planes and paint patterns with coins, and the Maps menu lets you fly through different worlds. You can also pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Hell difficulty.
