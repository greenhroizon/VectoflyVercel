About VectoFly

Updated 31 October 2026

Endless skies, one more flight

VectoFly is a free browser flying game at https://vectofly.com. Dodge obstacles, collect repair pills and fuel, earn coins and explore aircraft and worlds. It uses WebGL and runs without a download in compatible modern browsers.

Built on a creative foundation

The core game is based on The Aviator by Karim Maaloul (Codrops). VectoFly adds maps, aircraft, earned-coin unlocks, damage, fuel and a global leaderboard.

Help shape the game

Send gameplay suggestions, accessibility concerns and reproducible bug reports to contact@vectofly.com. Include your device, browser, orientation and steps to reproduce, without sharing personal or sensitive information.
