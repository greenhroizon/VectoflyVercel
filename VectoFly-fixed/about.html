About VectoFly

VectoFly is a free endless 3D flying game that runs in your browser - no download, no sign-up. Pick a plane, slide your thumb (or move the mouse) to dodge the obstacles and see how far you can fly. Every pilot's best score goes to the global leaderboard, so you are always racing the rest of the world.

What makes it fun

  • Made for touch. Slide a thumb anywhere to steer. The game adapts its camera and speed to your screen, so it plays well on small phones, tablets and big monitors.
  • Obstacles that think. Spike waves, walls with a gate, combs and orange homing spikes are built by a "director" that always leaves a fair way through and adapts to how you are doing.
  • Four difficulties (Easy, Medium, Hard, Hell) with bigger score multipliers as they get harder.
  • Planes, paints and maps to unlock. The first five planes and the first four maps are free from your very first flight; earn coins to unlock the rest.
  • A fair world leaderboard protected by signed run tickets and server-side checks.

Who makes it

VectoFly is made by a small independent team that loves arcade games. We read every email, and many features (touch steering, smarter spawns, free planes) came straight from player feedback. Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us at contact@vectofly.com or use "Report a bug / send feedback" at the bottom of the menu.

Credits and technology

  • Original concept and low-poly look: "The Aviator" by Karim Maaloul for Codrops (MIT licence). VectoFly extends it with new planes, maps, difficulties, a leaderboard and touch controls.
  • 3D graphics: Three.js (WebGL). Animation: GSAP TweenMax.
  • Hosting: Vercel. Leaderboard database: Google Firebase. Statistics: Google Analytics (you can opt out - see cookies).

Our promises

  • Free to play, no accounts, no real-money purchases.
  • We collect as little data as possible (see the Privacy Policy).
  • We keep the leaderboard clean: cheats are rejected and removed.
  • Pilot names are generated randomly, so no one can post their own text or personal information on the leaderboard.
