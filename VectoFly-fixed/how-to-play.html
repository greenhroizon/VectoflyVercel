How to Play

Updated 31 October 2026

Take flight

Enter a public nickname, choose a difficulty and press Play. New players start on Easy. Drag a finger anywhere on the sky to steer vertically; lifting and touching again re-anchors the gesture without jumping. On desktop use the mouse, arrows or WASD. Horizontal steering adjusts speed.

Survive and collect

Avoid red spikes. Each wave leaves a protected route; plan your moves early. Blue pills repair damage and earn coins, and orange cans restore fuel. Full damage or empty fuel ends a run. Difficulty and speed rise gradually. There are no UP or DOWN buttons.

Pause and progress

Tap the pause icon or press P to pause or resume; Space or Enter starts from the menu and M toggles audio. Switching away pauses your flight. The first five aircraft and four maps are free. Earn coins to unlock the rest and paint styles. Progress is saved on this browser; clearing storage may erase it.

Rankings

The menu and flight board show up to 50 global best scores. Visit the full leaderboard to browse all stored ranked players. One best entry per device-generated player ID is retained. Offline play is possible but global score submission requires a working score service.
