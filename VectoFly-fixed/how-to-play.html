How to Play VectoFly

Fly as far as you can. Dodge the red spikes, grab blue pills to repair, catch orange fuel cans to keep flying, and climb the world leaderboard. A run ends when your damage bar fills up or your fuel runs out.

Controls

DeviceHow to steer
Phone / tablet (touch)Put a thumb anywhere and slide up or down. The plane moves by the amount you slide, so there is no jump when you touch down and your thumb never covers the plane. Sliding left/right changes speed slightly. Works in portrait and landscape.
MouseThe plane follows the pointer: move up for height, left/right for speed.
KeyboardArrow keys or W A S D. Space start, P pause, M mute.

What you see

  • Red spikes - hitting one adds damage (the amount depends on difficulty). They come in scattered groups, full walls with a gap, combs, and spikes that bob up and down. There is always a way through.
  • Orange homing spikes - they follow your height and lock on shortly before impact. A flashing ! at the screen edge shows where one is coming from. Keep moving, then dodge at the last moment.
  • Blue pills - repair damage and add 5 points each. Trails often sweep up and down, so you have to fly to get them. Collect several in a row without a hit to build a combo.
  • Orange fuel cans - refill your fuel. Fuel slowly drains while you fly.

Scoring and coins

Score = (distance flown + 5 x pills) x difficulty multiplier.

DifficultyMultiplierFeel
Easyx1Slower start, gentle damage - great for learning
Mediumx1.25A bit faster, more spikes
Hardx1.5Fast, bigger waves, homing spikes more often
Hellx2Very fast, heavy damage, minimal mercy

All difficulties share one world leaderboard, which keeps your single best score. Your pilot name is generated randomly (you cannot type your own), and the New name button in the menu gives you a different one. There is no scoreboard while you fly: your results, rank and the world top 50 are shown in the menu. Coins are earned at the end of each flight, plus bonus coins for close calls (passing a spike by a hair), pill combos and a daily gift that grows with your streak.

Planes, paints and maps

The first five planes (Classic, Paper Plane, Falcon, Comet Floatplane, Tank Bomber) and the first four maps (Sunny Coast, Sunset Bay, Tropical Lagoon, Red Canyon) are free from your first flight. Spend coins in the Hangar and Maps menus to unlock more. Planes have perks such as armor, a pill magnet, lower fuel use or bigger fuel refills.

Tips for a high score

  1. Look for the gap, not the spikes. Waves always leave a gate that moves only a lane or two between waves.
  2. Do small moves early rather than big moves late.
  3. Do not park at the top or bottom: homing spikes find you there.
  4. Take pill trails when they are safe; they are worth points and repair damage.
  5. Grab fuel before it gets low - low fuel plays a warning sound.
  6. Harder difficulty means a bigger multiplier, but a clean Medium run often beats a crashed Hell run.

Frequently asked questions

How do I steer on a phone or tablet?

Put a thumb anywhere on the screen and slide up or down. The plane moves by the amount you slide, so your thumb never hides it. Sliding left or right changes your speed a little.

Is VectoFly free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and has no real-money purchases. The first five planes and first four maps are free from the start.

How is the score calculated?

Score = (distance flown + 5 for every pill) x the difficulty multiplier: Easy x1, Medium x1.25, Hard x1.5, Hell x2.

What are the orange spikes?

Homing spikes. They follow your height and lock on shortly before reaching you, so keep moving and dodge at the last moment. A flashing ! at the screen edge warns you where one is coming from.

Why does the game punish staying still?

Waves cover every height and staying at one height for a few seconds calls a homing spike, so there is no safe corner.

How do I unlock more planes and maps?

Earn coins at the end of each flight (plus bonuses for close calls, pill combos and a daily gift) and spend them in the Hangar and Maps menus.

Why was my score not saved?

Scores are checked by an anti-cheat system. Reload the page and play again. If it keeps happening, email contact@vectofly.com with your device and browser.

Can I choose my own pilot name?

No. Every pilot gets a randomly generated name such as Swift Falcon 42, so nobody can type their own and no personal information ends up on the leaderboard. Use the New name button in the menu to get a different random name.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

Use the Report a bug / send feedback link at the bottom of the menu, or email contact@vectofly.com.
