Finnish police open criminal probe into Google's forest clearing for data center

Investigators are examining clear-cutting that began before the legally required environmental assessment was complete.

Police in Finland have opened a criminal investigation into Google's clearing of forested land for a large data center, reported Silicon UK. The question is whether work started before the required environmental impact assessment was finished.

The case adds to growing scrutiny of how and where hyperscale campuses are built, alongside the ballot-box pushback seen in the United States.
